`expo-constants` provides system information that remains constant throughout the lifetime of your app's installation.

Your app can read these values, but it cannot change them. The device and the operating system provide some of them, such as `Constants.deviceName` and `Constants.systemFonts`. `Constants.expoConfig` contains most of your [app config](/workflow/configuration/#reading-configuration-values-in-your-app). To pass your own values to your app, add them to the [`extra`](/workflow/configuration/#extending-configuration) field of your app config. Then read them from `Constants.expoConfig.extra`.

Platform-specific values are in `Constants.platform`. For example, on iOS, `Constants.platform.ios.buildNumber` returns the build number of your app (it is `null` in Expo Go). For other device and app details, use [`expo-device`](./device/) and [`expo-application`](./application/).
