## Are apps built with Expo GDPR compliant?

**They can be! You can build GDPR compliant apps with Expo if you follow the requirements.**

While Expo ensures the proper handling and processing of developer data and end-user data, we cannot guarantee that the developers who build apps with Expo follow similar data privacy practices themselves.

## Data Processing Addendum, subprocessors, and data transfers

- **Data Processing Addendum (DPA)**: Expo provides a pre-signed DPA. To request a copy, [contact Expo support](https://expo.dev/support).
- **Subprocessors**: The vendors that process data for Expo are listed on the [subprocessors page](https://expo.dev/privacy/subprocessors). Expo gives notice of a new subprocessor by updating that page. To get each change as it is published, subscribe to the [RSS feed of subprocessor changes](https://expo.dev/privacy/subprocessors/rss).
- **International transfers**: Section 3.2 of the [Terms of Service](https://expo.dev/terms) states that Expo complies with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (C/2021/3972). The DPA also relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
