Expo Application Services (EAS) are the hosted building blocks of Expo's agentic mobile infrastructure. Agents and teams can use them to build, validate, deploy, and monitor their mobile apps.

Read the full pitch at [expo.dev/services](https://expo.dev/services), or follow the links below to learn how to get started.
